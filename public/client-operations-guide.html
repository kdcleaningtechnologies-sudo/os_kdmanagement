How FacilityOS works at your sites

What this note is

A plain-language guide to the live system — not a future proposal.

The client logs in to their portal and sees only their own contracted sites. KD can also generate the same monthly service PDF from Admin → Reports.

1. What this platform is

FacilityOS is the operations system for housekeeping, facility service, and security. It is not a generic HR tool. Every login is role-based:

You (client)

See your sites, staff on duty, attendance, inspections, complaints, security, contracts, invoices, and monthly PDFs.

Supervisor

Marks attendance, assigns tasks, inspects areas with photos, and handles site issues.

Worker / guard

Checks in and out with GPS, completes assigned tasks, and (guards) verifies patrols and visitors.

KD admin

Sets sites, roster, contracts, invoices, and generates the same monthly report you can print.

2. How staff location is taken

Location is captured automatically by the phone GPS when staff tap a button. They must allow location in the browser. We do not track anyone during the shift.

WhenWhat happensWhat you see
Worker / guard taps Check in or Check out Phone GPS is read once. The pin is compared to your site map pin and geofence (usually 200 metres, set per site). Attendance shows Verified if they are inside the circle, or Unverified if they punched from farther away. The punch is still saved.
Supervisor marks present / late Supervisor GPS is captured the same way and checked against the site pin. Same verified / unverified flag on the attendance record.
Guard marks a patrol checkpoint Verified GPS is stored on that checkpoint. Skipped checks do not take GPS. You can review security activity for your sites. You do not get a live moving map of the guard.

What this is not. There is no continuous tracking, no WhatsApp location share, and no live staff tracker on your portal. You see the contracted site map and whether today’s punches were geofence-verified.

3. How daily work actually runs

Attendance

  • Each person has a site and (usually) a shift on the roster.
  • Late is measured from that shift start plus grace — not a fixed 9:30.
  • Your Attendance page shows today’s on-site count, how many punches were GPS-verified, and a 30-day chart.

Tasks and checklists

  • Supervisors and KD assign work (daily / weekly / one-off) with a checklist.
  • Workers tick their own tasks. Recurring work clones the next occurrence when completed.
  • Your Overview shows this month’s task completion %.

Inspections and photos

  • Supervisor scores areas (cleanliness, washroom, floor, pantry, waste), adds photos, and can run Analyze photos.
  • AI returns a labelled suggestion: floor pass/fail, dust, bins, supplies, issues, recommended actions, and a suggested score.
  • The supervisor must apply or edit the score. The final mark is theirs. AI never auto-publishes a score.
  • You see the report, photos, and the AI suggestion clearly marked as AI-generated.

Complaints

  • You (or KD / supervisor) raise a ticket with category, priority, and photos.
  • Workflow: open → assigned → in progress → resolved → you confirm → closed.

Security

  • Guards check in like other staff, walk checkpoints, log incidents with photos, and sign visitors in/out.
  • Your Security page is read-only: incidents and recent visitors at your sites.

5. How to print the monthly PDF

  1. Sign in to the client portal.
  2. Open Reports.
  3. Tap Generate this month (KD can do this for you from Admin → Reports).
  4. On each site card, tap Print / Save PDF and choose “Save as PDF” in the browser dialog.

Allow pop-ups once. Use the same print dialog on this page to save this briefing as a PDF to email the client.

6. How SLA is calculated

SLA % is the average of two simple ratios for the period:

  • Share of inspections scoring 80 or above (supervisor’s final score).
  • Share of complaints that reached resolved (or later).

If one of those has no data, the other one is used. Contract SLA wording from your contract is printed on the monthly PDF when it is on file.
